## Overview

VolatilityForge is a quantitative finance research platform designed to forecast market volatility and evaluate its usefulness in systematic volatility-targeting strategies.

The system combines market-data processing, statistical and econometric baselines, machine-learning forecasts, statistical comparisons, volatility-regime analysis, and transaction-cost-aware backtesting.

SPY is the core asset, with VIX used as an implied-volatility reference. The forecasting pipeline evaluates next-day realized variance and cumulative realized variance over the next five trading days.

## Architecture

The high-level workflow is:

### Market-data pipeline

The pipeline downloads daily SPY and VIX data using yfinance, validates the raw datasets, aligns both assets by date, and constructs volatility features and forward targets.

Configuration is managed through `configs/config.yaml`. The pipeline produces raw and processed Parquet datasets for subsequent forecasting and evaluation.

### Volatility forecasting

The forecasting layer implements EWMA, GARCH, GJR-GARCH, HAR-RV, and LightGBM for one-day and five-day volatility forecasts.

HAR-RV serves as the primary benchmark. The system evaluates forecasts using QLIKE as the primary metric and mean squared error (MSE) as a secondary metric.

### Walk-forward evaluation

The evaluation pipeline uses chronological expanding-window walk-forward validation to preserve time ordering and prevent future information from entering model training.

Diebold–Mariano comparisons, HAC-lag sensitivity analysis, and volatility-regime analysis provide additional ways to assess differences between competing forecasts.

### Strategy backtesting

The strategy layer converts volatility forecasts into long-only exposure, subject to a maximum-leverage constraint, and applies transaction costs to changes in exposure.

Reports include gross and net returns, annualized return and volatility, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, exposure, and turnover. Sensitivity analysis evaluates volatility targets of 8%, 10%, and 12%, with transaction costs ranging from 0 to 20 basis points.

### Research dashboard

A Streamlit dashboard presents the project overview, forecast comparisons, volatility regimes, and strategy backtest results for interactive research and inspection.

## Engineering decisions

### Statistical baselines before machine learning

EWMA, GARCH-family models, and HAR-RV establish reference forecasts before more complex machine-learning approaches are considered. This allows model complexity to be evaluated against established alternatives.

### Leakage-aware evaluation

Expanding-window walk-forward validation preserves chronology. Multi-day targets must fully mature before they can be used for training or residual estimation.

### Separate forecast and strategy evaluation

Predictive accuracy does not guarantee better net strategy performance. The project therefore evaluates forecast metrics separately from exposure, turnover, transaction costs, and drawdown.

### Reproducible research

Configuration files, automated tests, saved reports, and documented commands support repeatable experiments. Historical data may still change when the data provider revises observations.

## Results

A separate five-day HAR-RV experiment evaluated Duan's smearing correction using historical out-of-sample log residuals whose targets had fully matured.

| Metric | Baseline HAR-RV | OOS-smearing HAR-RV |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mean QLIKE | 0.457635 | 0.372767 |
| MSE | 1.017897 × 10⁻⁶ | 9.917346 × 10⁻⁷ |
| Observations | 1,196 | 1,196 |

For the January 3, 2022, to October 8, 2026 development sample, the correction improved aggregate QLIKE by approximately 18.54% and MSE by approximately 2.57%.

However, the improvement was uneven across years and volatility regimes. Low-realized-variance performance deteriorated, and QLIKE improved on 496 of 1,196 individual dates. The correction remains experimental and has not been adopted in the production HAR-RV model.

These results do not establish statistical significance or future performance. The development period has already been used for model comparisons and strategy analysis, so it is not an untouched final holdout.

## What broke / constraints

Market-data revisions can change results between pipeline runs. Multi-day targets also require careful maturity checks to avoid information leakage.

Forecast improvements may vary across volatility regimes, and better statistical metrics do not necessarily translate into stronger net strategy returns after transaction costs.

The prospective evaluation period remains to be defined after model specifications, feature definitions, hyperparameters, and the evaluation protocol are frozen.

## Next

Potential future work includes freezing the evaluation protocol, defining a prospective final evaluation period, improving diagnostics and reproducibility, verifying remaining candidate models, and completing the source-to-guide scope audit.
