## Overview

MinutesMeet converts recorded meeting audio into structured meeting minutes.

The core pipeline uses two machine-learning stages.

The first stage performs automatic speech recognition with OpenAI Whisper. The resulting transcript becomes the input to a Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct model, which extracts the information needed for structured meeting documentation.

The generated output is formatted as Markdown minutes containing information such as attendees, discussion points, takeaways, and action items.

The core processing flow is:

```text
Audio Recording
      ↓
Whisper Speech Recognition
      ↓
Raw Transcript
      ↓
Prompt Construction
      ↓
Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct
      ↓
Structured Meeting Minutes
      ↓
Markdown Output
